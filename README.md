# Customer funnel and Ab testing for online stores
You work at a startup that sells food products. You need to research user behavior for the company's app.

## 🏁Objetive
Investigate the sales funnel, observe the results of an A/A/B test, study the results of the experiment and check if the difference between the groups is statistically significant.

## 🖥Technologies used
- **Python**: Pandas, plotly_express, streamlit
- **Jupyter Notebook**: Interactive environment for analysis.
- **CSV Dataset**: Contains user behavior data.

## 🔑Key Steps
1. **Data Description**:
   - Assessed the quality of the dataset and summarized its structure.
2. **Data Preprocessing**:
   - Handled missing values, corrected formatting issues, and removed duplicates.
3. **Study and verification of data**:
   - We will study the company's sales funnel.
4. **Test A/A/B**:
   - make a decision based on the results of a test.

## 🥇🏆Results
The analysis confirms that:
- The largest number of users are lost in the final stage of the process, as 76% of customers fail to complete this step when making a payment.
- Only 11.32% of our users complete the purchase process.
- Both groups 246 and 247 that were used for the A/A test at the time of analysis showed no differences in their clients or in their interaction with the platform.
- When analyzing the 3 groups, there is no significant difference that makes us reject the H0.
